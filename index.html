Troubleshooting

  • Take the earbuds out of the case and wait a few seconds.
  • Turn phone Bluetooth off and on, then try again.
  • If they won’t connect, forget them in system Bluetooth settings and pair again.
  • Use Chrome on Android — Web Bluetooth only works there.
  • The page must be opened over HTTPS (not as a raw file).
